A clean and modern modular UI.

VoltUI restyles unit frames, party and raid frames, nameplates, bars, bags, maps, tooltips and chat. Then it adds the small things that save you clicks, all set from one settings window. It looks right the moment you log in, and every part can be switched off.

01Quality of life
auto

Sells your junk

Open a vendor and your greys sell, one at a time, each slot checked again first. Chat says what you made. Hold Shift to skip it, and turn on repairs if you want them.

/volt

Search every setting

Type a word and the settings themselves show up, working, grouped by card. Colour finds color. Enter opens the first page it found.

/volt edit

Move frames to the pixel

A grid, snapping to the grid or to the frames nearby, arrow keys that nudge one pixel (Shift for ten), and X and Y you can type. Spots hold when you change resolution.

1 click

Copy from…

Give the pet frame the player frame's text, one bar another's layout, heal mode your raid frames, or friendly nameplates the enemy ones. Everything, text only, or the cards you pick.

/volt share

Your layout as a string

Export your layout and settings as letters and numbers that survive chat and forums. Pick the parts that go in, and undo the last import if you don't like it.

per character

Profiles

Named profiles, one per character if you like, each with every setting and every frame's spot. Until you make a second one, nothing changes.

/volt setup

Starting layouts

Classic, Centre, Healer and Top left. Try them as often as you like; Undo puts every frame back as it was before the first one.

/volt errors

An error catcher

Lua errors are kept with the values around them, and one button makes a report to copy, with versions and which parts are on. The game's own error window works as before.

bags

Bag search that knows words

Search names and types, or say boe, green, epic, quest, junk or new. Everything else dims.

bags

Leftover quest items

A small red bin on quest items no quest in your log needs any more, and on items that start a quest you've already done. Hover it for why.

chat

Copy from chat

A web address in chat opens a box to copy it from, and the small C in a chat window's corner opens its last 500 lines as plain text.

tooltips

Tooltips that answer

Item level, vendor price, how many you carry and how many are in the bank, who a unit is targeting, and ids while you hold Shift.

02Features

Built for how you actually play.

One window for every setting

Type /volt. Every setting VoltUI has is in one window, on pages under six headings, and changes show as you make them. There's nothing to apply.

  • Home has a tile for every part, with its switch and what it's doing right now.
  • Search finds a setting by its name, its choices or its hover text, and counts the hits on each page.
  • A setting that has no effect right now dims and says why, like Height with the power bar off.
  • Preview strips show a copy of the frame you're changing, since the window often covers the real one.
  • It opens in combat too. Only the settings the game locks in a fight wait for it to end.

Party and raid frames made for healing

VoltUI draws its own party and raid frames in place of the game's, with a heal mode that has a layout of its own. Click-casting works on any mouse button, with any modifier.

  • Incoming heals and shields on every bar, with an edge where they'd overheal.
  • Your HoTs and shields in the corners, with their time left.
  • Dispel windows and a warning with a sound when someone has something you can remove.
  • Range fading, missing health or percent, sorting, and a preview with made-up members to try it all on.

Bags that sort themselves

Press B as usual. One window holds every bag, in categories, with what you just looted at the top and every empty slot at the bottom. They're the game's own slots, so clicking, dragging and selling work as always.

  • Rarity outline and glow on every item, a gold ! on items that start a quest, and item levels if you want them.
  • Special bags kept apart: reagent bags, quivers, soul and profession bags, so your free count stays honest.
  • Filters and search, the same in the bank, which shows every tab at once.
  • Columns, slot size and every gap are yours to set.

A minimap that tells you more

The minimap goes square, with rows under it for the place you're in, your coordinates and the clock, then latency, frame rate, durability and gold. Move it and scale it like any other frame.

  • Readouts at a glance: latency, frame rate and durability turn amber, then red, when they need you.
  • Other add-ons' minimap buttons go in a drawer under the map. A tab says how many there are; click it to open or close.
  • The world map drags anywhere and resizes, with every zone's level range written over it.

Tooltips with the numbers you look for

Every tooltip gets the flat panel and VoltUI's font. Item tooltips take the item's rarity colour as their border, and units get a thin health bar with their health on it.

  • Item level on gear and the vendor price, when the game doesn't show them.
  • You have: how many you carry, and how many more are in the bank.
  • Class colours on names and who they're targeting ("You" when it's you).
  • Show them where the game does, at the cursor, or at a spot of your own, and hide unit tooltips in combat if you like.

04Plays well with others

It doesn't fight your other add-ons.

Steps aside

VoltUI's bags stay off while Baganator is on, and a part whose job another add-on is doing says so on Home instead of drawing over it.

Hooks, not replacements

VoltUI reuses and restyles the game's own frames and buttons wherever it can, so the game keeps working the way it expects to.

Errors kept quiet

VoltUI catches its own Lua errors and lists them in /volt errors instead of throwing a pop-up at you mid-fight.

Room to grow

Other add-ons can add settings pages, tooltip lines, minimap menu entries and /volt commands through VoltUI's hooks.

05Install

Three steps, then /volt.

  1. DownloadGet the newest zip from the releases page.
  2. Unzip into AddOnsPut the VoltUI folder in World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns, so that AddOns\VoltUI\VoltUI.toc exists.
  3. Start the gameA brand-new install offers you a starting layout once. Using Baganator? Untick it first, or VoltUI's bags stay off.
